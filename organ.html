Body Requirements & Lifestyle Answers

❓ Can people with smoking or drinking habits pledge organ donation?

YES! Having smoking or drinking habits does NOT prevent you from registering as an organ donor. Medical transplant teams evaluate each organ individually at the time of donation. For instance, even if a smoker's lungs are unfit, their corneas, kidneys, or liver may still be completely healthy and save lives!

❓ What body and medical conditions are required for organ donation?

Donors should generally be 18 years or older (or have parental consent if younger). The primary requirement is that pledged organs must be free from active untreated HIV, systemic active cancer, or active sepsis at the time of transplant matching.

❓ Why is family consent mandatory?

Under organ donation regulations, immediate next-of-kin family members are consulted to honor the donor's wish. Discussing your pledge with your family ensures your legal pledge pass is executed smoothly.
