Pre-Donation Eligibility & Habit Guidelines

Essential health rules before registering or donating blood

🍺
24-Hour Alcohol Rule Must be 100% alcohol-free for at least 24 hours prior to donation for donor & recipient safety.
🚬
2-3 Hour Smoking Timing Avoid smoking cigarettes for 3 hours before donation and 2 hours after donation to prevent dizziness.
⚖️
General Health Pre-Check Age 18-65 years, weight > 45kg, normal blood pressure, and free from active infections or fever.
