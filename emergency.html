🩸 Blood Donation Verified: Aug 2026

Who Can Donate Blood? Eligibility & Guidelines

Blood donation is a safe, life-saving act. Basic medical eligibility criteria ensure the health and safety of both the donor and the recipient.

✅ Helpful Choices & Preparation:
  • Age between 18 and 65 years, body weight >= 45 kg.
  • Normal hemoglobin level (at least 12.5 g/dL).
  • Drink 500ml water and eat an iron-rich meal 2 hours before donation.
⚠️ When to Postpone Donation:
  • Postpone if consumed alcohol within the last 24 hours.
  • Postpone if suffering from fever, active cold, or taking antibiotics.
🩺 When to Contact a Doctor:

Contact healthcare staff if persistent dizziness, fainting, or swelling occurs at the puncture site after donating.

📚 Source: Indian Red Cross Society & WHO
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
👩 Women's Health Verified: Aug 2026

Pregnancy Nutrition & Menstrual Iron Balance

Essential nutritional guidance focusing on iron-rich foods, folic acid, menstrual hygiene, and hydration during pregnancy and period cycles.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Consume spinach, lentils, jaggery, and dates for natural dietary iron.
  • Pair iron-rich foods with Vitamin C (lemons, oranges) to increase absorption.
  • Maintain hydration with 8-10 glasses of clean water daily.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Avoid unpasteurized milk products or raw unwashed produce during pregnancy.
  • Avoid unhygienic reusable cloths during period hygiene.
🩺 When to Contact a Doctor:

Seek immediate medical care for severe morning sickness, inability to retain fluids, abnormal bleeding, or severe abdominal pain.

📚 Source: ICMR & Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MOHFW)
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
🤒 Common Illnesses Verified: Aug 2026

Food & Hydration Self-Care During Fever & Cough

Practical supportive home care, hydration routines, and warning signs for common seasonal fever, sore throat, and mild colds.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Drink ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts), warm broths, and clear fluids frequently.
  • Eat light, easily digestible foods like warm oats, soups, and khichdi.
  • Warm saline gargles 3 times daily for sore throat relief.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Do NOT self-prescribe or consume unprescribed antibiotics.
  • Avoid heavy, greasy, or ice-cold foods during fever.
🩺 When to Contact a Doctor:

Seek urgent medical care if fever exceeds 102°F (38.9°C), lasts over 3 days, or is accompanied by severe headache, stiff neck, or shortness of breath.

📚 Source: WHO & AIIMS New Delhi
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
🥗 Nutrition Verified: Aug 2026

Balanced Diet Basics & Essential Nutrient Groups

Fundamentals of whole-food nutrition, dietary fiber, micronutrients, and daily meal planning for long-term health.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables and whole fruits.
  • Include plant-based proteins (pulses, beans, paneer) or lean meats.
  • Ensure adequate calcium intake through dairy, sesame, or fortified plant milks.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Minimize ultra-processed foods, deep-fried snacks, and sugar-sweetened beverages.
🩺 When to Contact a Doctor:

Consult a registered dietitian or doctor if experiencing severe nutritional deficiencies, sudden unexplained weight loss, or chronic digestive issues.

📚 Source: National Institute of Nutrition (NIN) & ICMR
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
❤️ Heart Health Verified: Aug 2026

Heart-Healthy Lifestyle & Dietary Sodium Control

Evidence-based habits for maintaining optimal blood pressure, healthy arterial function, and reducing cardiovascular risks.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Engage in 30 minutes of brisk walking or aerobic exercise at least 5 days a week.
  • Limit daily salt intake to under 1 teaspoon (<5 grams).
  • Include heart-friendly fats like mustard oil, walnuts, and flaxseeds.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Avoid tobacco smoking and exposure to secondhand smoke.
  • Avoid trans fats found in bakery shortenings and commercial fried snacks.
🩺 Emergency Warning Signs (Call 108):

Call 108 immediately for severe crushing chest pressure, pain radiating to left arm/jaw, or sudden shortness of breath.

📚 Source: World Heart Federation & WHO
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
🧒 Child Health Verified: Aug 2026

Child Care, Hydration & Pediatric Fever Awareness

Essential parent guidance on childhood hydration, routine vaccination schedules, and recognizing red-flag symptoms in young children.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Offer frequent sips of ORS, coconut water, or light soups during diarrhea or fever.
  • Ensure timely immunization according to the National Immunization Schedule.
  • Dress feverish children in light, breathable cotton clothing.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • NEVER administer aspirin to children or teenagers (risk of Reye's syndrome).
  • Do NOT over-wrap a child suffering from high fever.
🩺 When to Contact a Pediatrician:

Seek urgent medical care if an infant under 3 months has a fever >= 100.4°F (38°C), or if a child is unusually lethargic, unresponsive, or crying without tears.

📚 Source: UNICEF & Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP)
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
👴 Senior Health Verified: Aug 2026

Senior Nutrition, Bone Wellness & Fall Safety

Practical recommendations for older adults regarding bone density, proper hydration, medication safety, and home fall prevention.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Maintain calcium and Vitamin D intake through milk, curd, sunlight exposure, or physician advice.
  • Ensure well-lit hallways, non-slip bathroom mats, and clear walkways at home.
  • Drink fluids regularly even if thirst sensation decreases with age.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Avoid sudden posture changes from lying to standing to prevent orthostatic dizziness.
  • Never skip prescribed routine health checks or medication reviews.
🩺 When to Contact a Doctor:

Seek medical evaluation immediately following any fall, sudden joint immobility, confusion, or difficulty speaking.

📚 Source: National Institute on Aging & WHO
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
🧠 Mental Wellness Verified: Aug 2026

Stress Relief, Sleep Hygiene & Daily Mental Health

Evidence-based self-care strategies for managing daily stress, improving sleep quality, and fostering emotional resilience.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Maintain a consistent sleep routine aiming for 7-8 hours per night.
  • Practice 10 minutes of deep abdominal breathing or mindfulness daily.
  • Stay socially connected with family, friends, and supportive community networks.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Avoid screen usage (phones, TVs) at least 45 minutes before bedtime.
  • Avoid excessive caffeine or unguided self-medication for anxiety.
🩺 When to Seek Professional Support:

Contact a certified mental health professional if experiencing persistent sadness, severe anxiety, or feelings of overwhelm lasting over two weeks.

📚 Source: WHO Mental Health & NIMHANS
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
🩹 First Aid Verified: Aug 2026

Household First Aid: Minor Cuts, Burns & Nosebleeds

Step-by-step basic bystander first aid procedures for common non-emergency household injuries and thermal burns.

✅ Helpful Choices:
  • Minor Cuts: Clean gently with mild soap and cool running water; apply sterile gauze pressure.
  • Minor Burns: Hold burned area under cool running tap water for 10-15 minutes.
  • Nosebleeds: Sit upright, lean slightly forward, and pinch soft part of nose for 10 minutes.
⚠️ What to Avoid:
  • Do NOT apply ice, toothpaste, or butter to burns.
  • Do NOT tilt head backward during a nosebleed (prevents swallowing blood).
🩺 When to Seek Medical Attention:

Seek medical care for deep open wounds, burns larger than 2 inches, severe persistent nosebleeds, or signs of wound infection (pus, spreading redness).

📚 Source: Indian Red Cross Society & CDC
⚠️ Disclaimer: This information is provided for general educational purposes and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
